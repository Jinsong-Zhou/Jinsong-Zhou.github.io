With the rapid advancement of generative models, general-purpose generation has gained increasing attention as a promising approach to unify diverse tasks across modalities within a single system. Existing open-source frameworks often remain fragile and struggle to support complex real-world applications due to the lack of structured workflow planning and execution-level feedback.

We present **ComfyMind**, a collaborative AI system built on ComfyUI. It introduces a Semantic Workflow Interface that abstracts low-level node graphs into callable modules, and a search-tree planning mechanism with localized feedback execution.
