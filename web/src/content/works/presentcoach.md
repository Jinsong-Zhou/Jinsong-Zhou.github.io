Effective presentation skills are essential, yet learners often lack high-quality exemplars or personalized coaching. Existing AI tools typically score speech or generate scripts in isolation.

**PresentCoach** is a dual-agent system: an Ideal Presentation Agent turns slides into model presentation videos, and a Coach Agent evaluates the learner against those exemplars with multimodal analysis and Observation-Impact-Suggestion feedback, including a simulated audience.
