**ImpText-Reader** is the tool-augmented reader behind the ImpText paper: a three-stage training strategy that collaboratively optimizes tool selection and semantic reasoning so models can extract hidden or adversarial text.

See also the [ImpText paper](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/poster/63174).
