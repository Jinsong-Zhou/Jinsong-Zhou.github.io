Multimodal large language models are strong at standard text extraction, but they struggle with real-world **implicit text** — content concealed through physical deformation, visual camouflage, or cognitive suggestion. These techniques can bypass moderation and create safety risks.

We define Implicit Text Reasoning, introduce **ImpText-Bench**, and propose **ImpText-Reader**, a tool-augmented framework trained with a three-stage strategy that jointly optimizes tool selection and semantic reasoning.
