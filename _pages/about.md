<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>

Jinsong Zhou (周劲松) is a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the [AI Thrust, Information Hub](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/academics/hubs-and-thrust-areas/information-hub/) of [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Yingcong Chen](https://envision-research.hkust-gz.edu.cn). My work focuses on AI agents, multimodal reasoning, and generative systems. I am currently an AI Agent Solution Architect Intern at NVIDIA and a Co-Founder of [StoryVerse](https://storyverseai.art/). Previously, I was a Risk Control Algorithm Intern at ByteDance. I received my B.S. in Statistics from [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/).

# 💼 Experiences

- *2026.05 - Present*, **NVIDIA**, AI Agent Solution Architect Intern
  - Built 10 installable Skills for NVIDIA DevRel across major coding agents, standardizing delivery workflows.
  - Built a human-in-the-loop Agent Harness that automates expert-led Sim2Real modeling and tuning.
  - Built NIMHub, an NVIDIA model access layer covering 208 models and generating invocation code for 163 of them.
- *2025.08 - Present*, **[StoryVerse](https://storyverseai.art/)**, Co-Founder
  - Helped close a \$1M angel round at a \$10M valuation.
  - Built and led a six-person cross-functional team across product, UI/UX, frontend, backend, and machine learning; launched the multi-agent cinematic production Beta supporting 1,000+ concurrent users.
- *2025.05 - 2025.12*, **ByteDance, Douyin**, Risk Control Algorithm Intern
  - Built a LangGraph-based multi-agent risk assessment system that orchestrated 30+ MCP tools for autonomous violation detection and explainable evidence generation.
  - Created an adversarial image synthesis engine and 3,000+ tool-call training traces; trained Qwen3-VL-8B with SFT and GRPO for implicit-text reasoning.
