DeepSeek-R1 is an open-source large language model with advanced reasoning, low cost, and visible reasoning steps. Closed-source reasoning models limit customization and transparency in healthcare systems.

This perspective reviews the potential, applications, and challenges of DeepSeek-R1 in clinical decision support, patient engagement, and medical education.
